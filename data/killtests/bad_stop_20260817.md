# B4 — THE 35 "BAD-STOP FIRES": THE PREMISE IS LARGELY REFUTED — 2026-08-17

## FAILURE CONDITION (written FIRST)

This refutation is **WRONG** if any of the following is shown:

1. A convertible detector can emit a fire dict whose `stop`/`would_stop` is at or above its own
   `px`. Falsified by: finding one, in source or by execution. (A rig spec now pins the
   invariant — `SPEC_no_detector_emits_bad_stop` — so a future one goes RED at build time.)
2. `hidden_entry_step`'s `stop = min(l - 0.01, c * 0.95)` can exceed `c`. It cannot: both terms
   are strictly below `c` for `c > 0`.
3. The row field the study compared against (`price`) is in fact the fire price for the lanes
   in question, making the mismatch real rather than a pairing error.

## WHAT WAS ASKED, AND WHAT THE ROWS ACTUALLY SAY

The brief: *"today 35 fires had stop >= entry AT THE MOMENT OF FIRING (ma_pullback 17, hidden 9,
grinder 5, kevseq 2, v2conv 1, dip_rip 1) … a detector emitting a fire whose stop is at/above its
entry is emitting a malformed signal. DIAGNOSE per lane and FIX at the source."*

Reproducing the exclusion from the archive splits the 35 into **three different causes, none of
which is a detector emitting a bad stop.**

### Cause A — the study's OWN derived stop (17 fires: ma_pullback 17)

`exit_params_our_fires_20260817.py::derive_stop` uses the logged stop **when there is one**, and
otherwise **hand-rolls** it. `ma_pullback` rows carry no stop at all:

```
{"date":"2026-08-17","ma":"ema9","price":6.059,"recorded_at":"...","status":"triggered_ma_pullback","ticker":"MYSZ"}
```

so the study derives `conf-bar low × (1 − 1%)` from a **reconstructed 1-minute tape**. Where that
reconstruction put the confirmation bar's low above the fire price, the exclusion fired. **This is
a defect in the study, not in the lane** — and it is exactly the hazard rig section EG2 exists to
catch ("no hand-rolled detectors outside live_harness"). There is nothing to fix in `ma_pullback`
on this evidence. What `ma_pullback` genuinely lacks is a **logged stop**, which is an
observability gap, not a malformed signal.

### Cause B — the row pairs a LIVE QUOTE with a FIRE-BAR stop (9 fires: grinder 5, kevseq 2, v2conv 1, dip_rip 1)

These rows carry **both** `fire_px` (the detector's fire price) and `price` (the live quote at
gate time). The study used `price` as the entry reference. Against their **own** fire price every
one of the nine stops is valid:

| lane | ticker | time | `fire_px` | `stop` | `price` (live) | stop < fire_px? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| grinder | XPON | 11:14:24 | 4.72 | 4.67 | 4.58 | **yes** |
| grinder | WOK | 11:14:25 | 2.7373 | 2.59 | 2.559 | **yes** |
| grinder | WTM | 11:58:33 | 2131.70 | 2130.66 | 2121.55 | **yes** |
| kevseq | RPGL | 11:46:57 | 2.64 | 2.55 | 2.55 | **yes** |

Recomputed against `fire_px`, **stop ≥ fire price occurs 0 times** in these four lanes.

What these nine rows really record is **the live quote falling below the fire price and through
the stop between the fire bar and the gate** — i.e. *negative* entry drift. That is item **B1**
seen from the other side, and B1's fix (fire at the fill bar's close) plus its `degenerate_stop`
refusal is the correct response to it. It has been shipped.

### Cause C — a real pairing defect in the hidden_entry ROW (9 fires: hidden 9)

`hidden_shadow_fire` rows have `stop ≥ price` in 9 cases (MYSZ, SLE ×2, IVF ×2, PFSA, DAIC,
IPST, WFF). Here there is no `fire_px` to fall back on — so this one needed source.

`hidden_entry_step` computes `stop = min(l - 0.01, c * 0.95)` and `px = round(c, 4)`. **Both
terms of that `min` are strictly below `c`**, so the detector's stop is *always* below its own
fire price. The detector is clean.

The defect is at the **logging site**:

```python
_hpx = price if price and price > 0 else _he_fire.get("px") or 0
_log_decision(t, "hidden_shadow_fire", price=_hpx, stop=_he_fire["stop"], ...)
```

The row pairs the **live quote** (`price`) with the **fire bar's** stop. When the quote has
drifted below the fire bar's close, the row reads as `stop ≥ entry` even though the signal was
never malformed. MYSZ 04:40: anchor 5.6696, stop 5.491 (≈ anchor × 0.968 — the wick low), live
quote 5.45. The fire bar's close was above 5.491; the quote had fallen through it.

**This is the same defect class as B1 and Cause B: a row whose `price` is not its fire price.**
The one-field fix — stamp `fire_px=_he_fire["px"]` on the row — is **decision-row logging**, which
this session does **not** own: a concurrent agent holds the logging/archive/harness side. It is
handed off below rather than edited from two directions at once.

## WHAT SHIPPED FOR B4

No detector change — because the diagnosis does not support one, and shipping a
`bad_stop_refused` refusal into lanes that cannot produce the condition would be adding a branch
that can never be taken to a money path. What shipped instead is the **invariant, pinned**:

`rig/test_batchB_20260817.py::SPEC_no_detector_emits_bad_stop` asserts that every convertible
detector carries its degeneracy guard at the source —
`v2_pullback_step` (`V2_MINSTOP_PCT` floor), `grinder_shadow_step` (`if not (lo15 < c)`),
`bandpass_step` (`if stop < c and …`), `hidden_entry_step` (`min(l - 0.01, c * 0.95)`),
`kevseq_step` (`why.append("degenerate_stop")`). A future detector that emits a fire with an
invalid stop makes the build RED. This spec **passed at the parent commit** — correctly, since
the invariant already held; it is a regression pin, and it is labelled as one rather than
dressed up as a fix.
